Method

Active copy — Sierra status can satisfy a request: on shelf, checked out, on holdshelf, in transit, in processing. Withdrawn, lost, missing, library-use-only, on-order and consortial-request statuses are excluded. Read live from the circulating_status seed used by the collection_pressure mart.

Exclusive — active copies across the branch’s 56 shelf locations equal active copies system-wide. Measured per branch, not per shelf: one copy in Fiction plus one in Large Print is still 100% held here.

Demand — circ_365 is checkouts in the trailing 365 days; circ_prior is the same window one year earlier. Trend is the difference. Checkouts only: Sierra records check-ins, renewals, hold placements and filled holds in the same table, and only op_code = 'o' is a checkout.

Demand is per TITLE, not per copy. It counts checkouts of the title anywhere in the system over the window — including copies that have since been withdrawn, which is often how the title came to be exclusive here in the first place. It is not a count of how often the surviving copy on this branch’s shelf went out. Read it as “how much this title has been wanted”, which is the question a rescue decision turns on; do not read it as this copy’s own circulation history.

Shelf areas — derived, not Sierra-native. location_grouping.shelf_group is populated only for Main Library; at branches it mirrors the audience and main_zone is NULL. Areas come from a hand-owned file of ordered keyword rules over the shelf name. An unmapped shelf fails the build rather than landing in an “Other” bucket.

Order within an area — call number, natural-sorted (100 → 520 → 741.5).

Not the published mart. collection_pressure gates on demand ≥6/yr and rising year-over-year, which excludes flat-but-busy titles that disappear just the same. It covers 47 of these 767; of the 720 omitted, 4 circulate 12+/yr. Population rebuilt from item.records using the mart’s active-status definition.

Counts — 770 titles met the exclusivity test; 3 dropped as suppressed or deleted bibs, leaving 767.

Sources — item.records, circ_trans.transactions, reference.location_grouping, 2026-08 mint. No patron data.

Timestamps — all times EDT (host local). Data queried is when the lake was read and is the report’s currency; report generated is when this document was written. They differ when a cached dataset is re-rendered.
